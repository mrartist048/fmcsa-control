Send campaigns from your own email

Your emails go out one by one, at the pace you choose, even when this page is closed. Send with Gmail / Google Workspace, or with Zoho, Outlook, hosting (cPanel) or any other email account. Contacts and settings stay in your own Google Sheet.

Send with

Your domain email (for example sales@yourdomain.com) can send from Google too, 24/7, with your computer off and no Mailer. Add it once in Gmail: Settings, Accounts and Import, Send mail as, Add another email address (untick "Treat as an alias", enter your host's SMTP server). Then press Reload and pick it here. Replies arrive in that mailbox; forward them to this Gmail so a reply stops the follow-ups.

Passwords: Zoho, Outlook and Yahoo usually need an app password (always when two-factor login is on). Hosting mail (Hostinger, cPanel, GoDaddy) uses the normal mailbox password. Emails go out only while the Mailer is running, so use a computer that stays on, or a small server (VPS).

How to set it up (5 minutes, once)

Create the Google Sheet that stores your campaign (in Gmail mode it also sends the emails).

  1. Create a new Google Sheet. Open sheets.new and name it "Dispatch Link Campaign". It is only for campaigns.
  2. Open Apps Script. In that sheet click Extensions, then Apps Script. Delete everything in Code.gs.
  3. Paste the code. Press Copy Apps Script code below, paste it in Code.gs and press Save (Ctrl+S).
  4. Allow it once. In the toolbar choose the function authorize and press Run. Google asks for permission to send and read Gmail (Gmail mode uses it to send and to notice bounces and replies; in "Other email" mode it is not used, but you still allow it). If it says "not verified" choose Advanced, then Go to project, then Allow.
  5. Deploy it. Click Deploy, New deployment, type Web app, Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone, press Deploy. Copy the Web app URL (it ends with /exec) into the box above and press Connect.
Changed the code later? Deploy, Manage deployments, pencil icon, Version: New version, Deploy. The URL stays the same.

Write the emails
One first email and up to 4 follow-ups. A follow-up is sent only if the person has not replied, bounced or unsubscribed.

        

          

            

            
One email per lead. As soon as it is sent the lead is deleted; only the address is kept so it is never emailed twice.

            

              
Sender details

              
The business address and the unsubscribe line are added to the bottom of every email. This is required by US law (CAN-SPAM).

              

                

                

              

              
              
              
              
              

                
Email signature
A small professional card under every email: logo, name, contact and a green WhatsApp button. The email text stays plain, so it still lands in the inbox.

                  

                

                  

                    

                    

                    

                    

                    

                    

                  

                  
                  

                    

                    

                      

                    

                  

                  
                  
Tip: end your email text with a short sign-off such as "Thanks," and leave out your name and company: the signature adds them.

                

              

              

              

            

          

          

            

              
Preview

              
Placeholders: {name} owner full name, {firstname} first name, {company} carrier name. If the name is unknown it becomes "there". Variations: {Hello|Hi|Hey} picks one at random for each email, which looks more natural to spam filters.
